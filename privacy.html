PRIVACY POLICY

Effective October 10, 2026 · LienVault

LienVault is a small, independent research service operated by one person in California. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you use this website or subscribe, and what happens to it.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Your email address — when you subscribe through Gumroad, Gumroad collects your email so your access link can be delivered and your subscription managed. We can see the email address attached to your subscription.
  • Payment information — payments are processed entirely by Gumroad. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic technical data — this page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers. We do not run advertising trackers or sell visitor data.

HOW IT'S USED

  • To deliver and maintain your subscription access.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.
  • To send subscription-related messages (receipts and access information are sent by Gumroad).

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not share your information with third parties except Gumroad (checkout, delivery, and subscription management) and as required by law.

YOUR CHOICES

You can cancel your subscription at any time in your Gumroad library, which stops future charges. California residents may also email us to ask what personal information we have about them, or to ask that it be deleted, and we will honor reasonable requests.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
